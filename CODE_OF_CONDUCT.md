# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Stratus welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private information, credentials, and security reports.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue when the report contains personal or sensitive information. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when a person is publicly representing the project.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
